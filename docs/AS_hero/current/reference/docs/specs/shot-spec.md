# AS hero: shot spec (APPROVED 2026-11-16, still only; layout v4 approved)

Frame for the approved home layout A (Paper "07 — Anti-slop home", hero 1440 x 860).
This is the still / poster frame. Motion is decided after the still is approved.

## Frame

- Size: 1440 x 860 (about 5:3), full bleed. Safe crop to 16:9 and to a phone portrait crop
  that keeps AS.
- Copy zone: left 55%, top 60% stays calm sky (headline, subline, Let's talk).
- AS: right third, centre line at about 72% of the width. Head top about 12% from the top.

## Subject

**2026-11-17, Amit:** the end frame is now a **medium shot, closed frame** (waist up), tighter
than the knees-up framing below. See `motion-spec.md`.


- AS from the locked model sheet, three-quarter front view, facing left toward the copy.
- Framing: knees up (the frame cuts just below the knees).
- Pose: standing still, relaxed, hands empty at his sides, cape hanging with a light lift
  to the right. No props.

## Camera

- Height: at his waist, looking slightly up (about 8 degrees), so he rises against the sky.
- Lens: about 35 mm equivalent: mild perspective, no wide-angle distortion on the figure.
- Static. Horizon at about 68% down the frame (in the lower third, behind his thighs).

## World

- AS stands on the roof of one approved Browsercity tower. Its roof edge and orange chrome
  run across the bottom right under him.
- Around him: approved towers below and beside him. Nearer towers show their orange chrome
  tops in the lower left. Two or three towers on the right rise above his waist line, for scale.
- Distant skyline: the same towers, smaller and fading into haze at the horizon.
- Scale: one floor is about 3.5 m; AS is 1.85 m. Window panes read at architectural size
  next to him, never toy-sized.

## Light and sky

- Sky: pale blue at the top to warm cream at the horizon (the style palette).
- Sun: low, behind the city on the right, just above the horizon, partly hidden by AS.
  It gives him the peach rim on the right edge (as on the model sheet).
- Haze: cream haze over the distant skyline only. No haze on AS or the near towers.
- No sun rays crossing the copy zone.

## Style

`style.md`: flat screen-print blocks, no outlines, grain, the five-colour palette.

## Never

No other people, no text or logos except the AS badge, no binary code, no props, no
lens flares, no glossy 3D look.

## How it will be made (proposal)

1. Blender block-out: the approved towers at real scale, a 1.85 m AS stand-in, this camera.
   Flat-colour render = the exact layout. Free.
2. Amit approves the layout render.
3. Final art: either a flat-colour Blender render plus grain (no AI), or a paint-over of the
   layout with AS added from the model sheet (AI, priced and approved first).
